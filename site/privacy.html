Privacy

This site. This site uses no cookies, analytics or ads, and has no forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process access logs (IP address, time, page) for security and operations.

The app. Screenwall processes documents on your computer. Detection, masking and auditing are local; it does not send uploaded documents to us. The app interface connects to a local server running on your computer. If you choose optional cloud chat, only the masked text you approve is sent, with your own account, to the provider you pick (Anthropic or OpenAI) under that provider's terms. The text is shown to you before every question is sent.

Email. hello@screenwall.site is a contact address that forwards incoming messages through Spaceship to Gmail. Messages are used to respond to your contact request. Incoming forwarding does not provide a separate outgoing mailbox.
